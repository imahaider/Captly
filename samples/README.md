# Samples

`two_cities_16k.wav` is the opening of *A Tale of Two Cities*, 44.4 s at 16 kHz mono. It comes from `test-assets/` in the [Moonshine repo](https://github.com/moonshine-ai/moonshine) at tag v0.1.5, which is MIT licensed. `two_cities.txt` is the matching public-domain text, for measuring WER with `tools/Bench`.
