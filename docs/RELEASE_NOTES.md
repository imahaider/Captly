The first release of Captly: live captions that stick to your video.

**Download** the zip for your PC, unzip it anywhere and run `Captly.exe`:

- `win-x64` for Intel and AMD PCs
- `win-arm64` for Snapdragon and other ARM64 PCs

Setup downloads a speech model once (43 MB for the smallest), and Captly then works offline. Each zip has a `.sha256` file to check it against.

**What's in it**

- Captions for one app, such as your browser, so music and notifications stay out
- Captions pinned to the video window, through moves, resizes and fullscreen
- Pause and seek awareness, from Windows' media controls
- Text and subtitle transcripts, named after each video
- A tray popover, full settings, light and dark themes, and global shortcuts

**Known limits**

- English only.
- Not code-signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
- Runs on the processor, not the graphics card.
- Tested on one Snapdragon X Plus PC with Windows 11. Intel and AMD PCs have not been tested by hand yet.
- A YouTube ad that changes the video title starts its own transcript.
